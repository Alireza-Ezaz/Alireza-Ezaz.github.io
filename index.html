Software engineer & AI-focused full-stack developer

I build web applications, REST APIs, agentic AI prototypes, and data-driven evaluation workflows. I co-founded SevenTask, designed its Angular and .NET architecture, and shipped core product features — Kanban board, calendar, authentication, chat, and API docs — scaling it to 200+ monthly active users.

At Cohere, I evaluate and debug AI-generated coding responses across many languages and support a 40-person annotator team with calibration feedback. I recently built CaseLogic, a source-grounded AI legal research assistant that placed 3rd at the EvenUp × OpenClaw Hackathon.

On the research side, my M.Sc. thesis TAAF combines knowledge graphs and LLMs for trace analysis and was accepted to ICSE 2026. I've published four peer-reviewed papers and presented at the Tracing Summit 2025 to an audience of engineers from Google, Ciena, and Ericsson.
